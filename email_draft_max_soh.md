## Why each piece is there (strategy notes)

- **Subject** names the Explainer Series — Max runs it; instant relevance, no "collaboration inquiry" vagueness.
- **First paragraph** quotes their own Future Plans line back — the hook decided in the handover.
- **"Designed to sit comfortably within DPA's visual style"** — signals care without critiquing the site. The contrast findings (#7A7A7A at 4.29:1, light-green active links) stay UNSAID until conversation #2.
- **"Practises what it shows"** — the accessibility specs are stated as properties of the artifact, not as Echo's claims about herself. The artifact is the CV.
- **CSV append line** plants the sustainability idea (annual updates) without proposing site integration — that presumption stays out of email #1.
- **One line lived experience**, framed as a working strength (async), not as identity-first.
- **No paid ask, no meeting ask** — reply-friendly close; the only implicit ask is "say this is useful."
- **"Thank you either way"** releases pressure; charity staff get many asks.
